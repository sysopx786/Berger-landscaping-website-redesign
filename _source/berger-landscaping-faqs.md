## 1. Mowing

**1. How often will you mow my lawn?**
Most lawns do best with a regular weekly visit during the growing season. We can adjust the schedule for slower growth in spring and fall or for your property's needs.

**2. What is included in each mowing visit?**
Mowing, edging along walkways and beds, trimming around obstacles, and blowing clippings off driveways and hard surfaces.

**3. Do you mow residential and commercial properties?**
Yes. We serve homes, businesses, and multi-living communities.

**4. Can you mow a large lawn or a steep hill?**
Yes. We take on challenging properties, including large lawns and steep hills.

**5. Do I need to be home when you mow?**
No. As long as we can reach the lawn, you don't need to be there. Just let us know about locked gates or pets.

**6. What if it rains on my mowing day?**
We'll reschedule for the next suitable day. Cutting very wet grass can tear it and leave ruts.

**7. How short do you cut the grass?**
We cut at a height suited to your grass type and the season. Mowing too short stresses a lawn and invites weeds and heat damage.

**8. Do you bag the clippings?**
We can discuss this when you set up service. By default, clippings are cleared from driveways, walkways, and hard surfaces.

**9. Can you get rid of weeds in my lawn while you mow?**
Regular, properly timed mowing helps crowd out weeds. For heavy weed problems, ask us about the best approach for your lawn.

**10. Do I have to sign a contract for mowing?**

**11. How do I get a mowing quote?**

**12. Can you start mid-season?**
Usually yes. Contact us with your address and we'll let you know when we can add you to the schedule.

**13. Will you mow around my flower beds, trees, and play equipment?**
Yes. We trim and edge around beds, trees, fences, and other obstacles as part of the visit.

**14. Do you offer one-time mowing, such as before an event or a home sale?**

## 9. Tree Planting

**1. What kinds of trees do you plant?**
Shade and ornamental trees and evergreens, like the ones in our gallery. Ask us about specific varieties for your property.

**2. Do you supply the trees, or can I buy my own?**
Please ask. Call or text (610) 420-3338 and we'll explain how we handle sourcing.

**3. When is the best time to plant a tree?**
In Pennsylvania, spring and fall are generally the best planting windows, when temperatures are mild. We'll recommend timing for your trees.

**4. Why do you put a mulch ring around new trees?**
A mulch ring holds moisture, keeps weeds down, moderates soil temperature, and keeps mowers and string trimmers away from the trunk.

**5. Can mulch be piled up against the trunk?**
No. Mulch piled against the bark (sometimes called a "mulch volcano") holds moisture on the trunk and can cause rot. Mulch should be spread wide and kept back from the bark.

**6. Can you plant evergreens for privacy?**
Yes. We plant rows of evergreens along fence lines and property edges to create a screen.

**7. How far apart should privacy evergreens be planted?**
It depends on the variety and how quickly you want a full screen. We'll recommend spacing based on the trees you choose.

**8. How often should I water a new tree?**
New trees need regular, deep watering through their first growing season, and more during dry spells. Ask us for guidance on your specific trees.

**9. Do you guarantee the trees you plant?**
Ask us about our current terms.

**10. Can you remove a dead or unwanted tree?**
We remove plants and shrubbery as part of our trimming service. For large trees, tell us about the situation and we'll let you know what we can handle or whether a specialist is needed.

**11. Can you trim my existing trees?**
Yes. See our Trimming service.

**12. Do you need to check for buried utilities before digging?**

## 10. Aeration, Sod & Seeding

**1. How soon can I use my new lawn?**
That depends on whether you choose seed or sod, and on the weather. We'll give you a realistic timeline before we start.

**2. When is the best time to seed?**
Early fall is usually the best window. Spring can work too. We'll recommend timing for your yard.

**3. When is the best time to aerate?**
Fall is the top choice, with spring as a second option. Once a year is a good rule of thumb, and fall lets you pair aeration with overseeding.

**4. Should I pick up the soil plugs after aeration?**
No. Leave them on the lawn. They break down within a week or two and work back into the soil.

**5. Can you repair just part of my lawn?**
Yes. Patching bare or thin areas is a common job.

**6. Does the ground need work before seeding or sod?**
Often, yes. New lawns usually need soil prep and some grading first. If water pools in your yard, tell us about it so we can plan around it.

**7. Should I choose seed or sod?**
Seed costs less but takes weeks to fill in. Sod costs more and gives you a finished-looking lawn right away. We'll walk you through the tradeoffs for your yard and budget.

**8. How much does aeration, seeding, or sod cost?**
It depends on the size of the area, its condition, and whether you choose seed or sod. Call or text (610) 420-3338 for a quote.

**9. How should I water new seed?**
Water right after seeding, then keep the soil surface damp, not soggy, for about three weeks. After that, water more deeply but less often, ideally early in the morning.

**10. How should I water new sod?**
New sod needs frequent watering at first so its roots knit into the soil below. We'll give you a watering plan when it's installed.

**11. When can I mow new grass?**
Wait until it's about 4 inches tall. Use a sharp blade and cut no more than a third of the height at a time.

**12. Can you aerate and overseed in the same visit?**
Yes. Seed takes best right after aeration, so the two are often done together.

**13. Do you offer this for businesses and HOAs?**
Ask us. We serve residential, commercial, and multi-living properties.

**14. How do I handle mowing after new seed or sod?**
Ask us how to coordinate mowing with your new lawn so young grass isn't damaged.

**15. How do I get a quote?**
Call or text (610) 420-3338 or email drew@bergerlandscaping.net. Photos of the area and a rough size help us quote it.
