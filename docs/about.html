About us

Family-owned, rooted in Southeastern PA

Berger Landscaping is a family-owned landscaping business proudly serving the communities of Southeastern Pennsylvania.

Meet Drew Berger

Welcome to Berger Landscaping LLC! The company was founded by Drew Berger, a devoted Penn State alumnus. It's built on a foundation of experience in business management and a passion for creating beautiful outdoor spaces. Drew is dedicated to delivering meticulous and personalized landscaping solutions for every project.

Outside of work, he enjoys time spent with his wife and three children, serves as a deacon at a local church, and is an avid hunter and fisherman. We deeply appreciate your ongoing support, which helps our growing family and allows us to make a positive impact in our community.

Thank you for being an essential part of the Berger Landscaping LLC family!
