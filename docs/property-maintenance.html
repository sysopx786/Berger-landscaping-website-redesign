About property maintenance

Property maintenance ensures that every outdoor space (whether a residential home, commercial business, or multi-living community) remains clean, safe, and visually appealing throughout the year. It includes the ongoing care of lawns, landscape beds, shrubs, trees, hardscapes, and common areas to protect your investment and keep the property looking its best in every season.

From routine mowing and trimming to seasonal cleanups, mulch installation, and debris removal, property maintenance provides consistent, reliable upkeep that enhances curb appeal and supports long-term landscape health.

All FAQs by service
What does property maintenance cover?

Ongoing care of lawns, landscape beds, shrubs, trees, hardscapes, and common areas, so your property stays clean, safe, and good-looking year-round.

Who is property maintenance for?

Homeowners, commercial businesses, and multi-living communities.

How is it different from just mowing?

Mowing is one piece. Property maintenance adds services such as trimming, bed upkeep, seasonal cleanups, mulch, and debris removal for the whole property.

Can you build a plan that fits my property and budget?

Yes. We'll talk through what you need and put together a plan around it.

Do you handle mulch installation?

Yes. Mulch installation is part of the maintenance services we offer.

Do you take care of seasonal cleanups?

Yes. Seasonal cleanups, such as spring and fall, are part of ongoing maintenance.

Can you maintain common areas for an HOA or apartment community?

Yes. We care for common areas at multi-living communities and commercial properties.

How often will you visit?

It depends on the plan. Many properties are visited weekly in the growing season, with other tasks scheduled as needed.

Do you maintain hardscapes like patios and walkways?

Hardscape upkeep is part of overall property care. Tell us what you have and we'll include it in the plan.

Do you remove debris after storms?

Yes, debris removal is part of our property maintenance. Contact us and we'll work you in as soon as we can.

Will the same crew come each time?

We aim for consistency so the crew knows your property. Ask us about specifics when you sign up.

Does maintenance help my property's long-term health?

Yes. Consistent upkeep protects your investment and supports lawn, plant, and tree health over the years.

How do I get started?

Call or text (610) 420-3338, or use the quote form on our Contact page for a quote.

Can I add or change services later?

Yes. Your needs change with the seasons, and we can adjust the plan as they do.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Pottstown, PA and nearby towns, including Royersford, Spring City, Phoenixville, Boyertown, and Gilbertsville. See the full list on our Service Area page.
