About mowing

A well-kept lawn is the foundation of a beautiful property. Our mowing service delivers clean, consistent cuts on a regular schedule, so your lawn stays healthy, even, and inviting all season long.

Each visit includes mowing, edging along walkways and beds, trimming around obstacles, and blowing clippings off driveways and hard surfaces. We cut at the right height for your grass type, which helps it grow thicker, resist weeds, and handle summer heat.

We also take on challenging properties, including large lawns and steep hills. Whether you manage a home, a business, or a multi-living community, we show up on time and leave your property looking its best.

Mowing FAQs

All FAQs by service
How often will you mow my lawn?

Most lawns do best with a regular weekly visit during the growing season. We can adjust the schedule for slower growth in spring and fall or for your property's needs.

What is included in each mowing visit?

Mowing, edging along walkways and beds, trimming around obstacles, and blowing clippings off driveways and hard surfaces.

Do you mow residential and commercial properties?

Yes. We serve homes, businesses, and multi-living communities.

Can you mow a large lawn or a steep hill?

Yes. We take on challenging properties, including large lawns and steep hills.

Do I need to be home when you mow?

No. As long as we can reach the lawn, you don't need to be there. Just let us know about locked gates or pets.

What if it rains on my mowing day?

We'll reschedule for the next suitable day. Cutting very wet grass can tear it and leave ruts.

How short do you cut the grass?

We cut at a height suited to your grass type and the season. Mowing too short stresses a lawn and invites weeds and heat damage.

Do you bag the clippings?

We can discuss this when you set up service. By default, clippings are cleared from driveways, walkways, and hard surfaces.

Can you get rid of weeds in my lawn while you mow?

Regular, properly timed mowing helps crowd out weeds. For heavy weed problems, ask us about the best approach for your lawn.

Do I have to sign a contract for mowing?

Ask us about our current terms. Call or text (610) 420-3338 and we'll explain how service works.

How do I get a mowing quote?

Call or text (610) 420-3338, or use the quote form on our Contact page with your address and a bit about your lawn.

Can you start mid-season?

Usually yes. Contact us with your address and we'll let you know when we can add you to the schedule.

Will you mow around my flower beds, trees, and play equipment?

Yes. We trim and edge around beds, trees, fences, and other obstacles as part of the visit.

Do you offer one-time mowing, such as before an event or a home sale?

Please ask. We can often help with a one-time cut when our schedule allows.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Berger Landscaping LLC is fully licensed and insured.
