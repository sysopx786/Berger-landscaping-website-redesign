Privacy Policy

What this website collects, and what it doesn't.

Last updated October 11, 2026

Summary

This site does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies. It does not ask you for an account, and it does not collect personal information unless you choose to contact us.

Information stored in your browser

If you switch between light and dark themes, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it. If you choose to load the Google Map, that choice is remembered the same way. You can clear both at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Google Maps

The map on our Contact page does not load until you press “Load Google Map.” When it loads, Google receives your IP address and device information and may set cookies, under Google's Privacy Policy. Links to Google Maps and Google reviews open Google's own site.

Facebook

We link to our Facebook page. We do not embed Facebook content on this site, so Facebook receives nothing from your visit here unless you follow a link to it.

Phone, text, and email

Call, text, and email buttons open apps on your own device. When you contact us, we use the details you send (such as your name, number, address, and photos) only to respond, quote, and do the work you ask for. We do not sell your information.

Online quote form

This site does not currently offer an online form. If one is added, this policy will be updated to say where submissions are sent and how long they are kept.

Fonts

Typefaces are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser requests them.
