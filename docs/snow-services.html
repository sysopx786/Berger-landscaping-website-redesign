About snow services

At Berger Landscaping LLC, we prioritize keeping your property safe in winter. Our snow plowing and removal services efficiently clear snow from driveways and commercial lots, ensuring safe surfaces.

We also offer salting services to reduce slips and falls using effective salt treatments. Ask about our pet-safe ice-melt options that protect your pets while ensuring traction for walkways.

All FAQs by service
What snow services do you offer?

Snow plowing and removal for driveways and commercial lots, plus salting.

Do you serve residential and commercial properties?

Yes. We clear driveways and commercial lots.

What does salting do?

It helps reduce slips and falls by treating surfaces with effective salt.

Do you offer pet-safe ice melt?

Yes. Ask about our pet-safe ice-melt options that protect your pets while giving walkways traction.

When do you plow?

We aim to clear surfaces promptly after snowfall. Ask us about timing for your property.

How much snow triggers a plow visit?

That depends on your agreement and property type. Please ask when you sign up.

Can you clear walkways and steps?

Please ask about walkway and step clearing for your property.

Do you plow overnight or early morning?

Ask us about timing, especially for businesses that need to be open early.

Do I need a seasonal contract?

Please ask about our current options, including seasonal or per-visit service.

Do you remove snow as well as push it?

Yes. We offer both plowing and removal.

Will you plow around parked cars?

We'll do our best. Please let us know your parking situation so we can plan.

How do I get a snow service quote?

Call or text (610) 420-3338, or use the quote form on our Contact page.

Are you insured for snow work?

Yes. We are fully licensed and insured.

Can I sign up in the middle of winter?

Often, yes, depending on availability. Contact us.

Is pet-safe ice melt safer for my lawn and driveway too?

Ice melt choices vary. Ask us about the options and what's best for your surfaces and your pets.
