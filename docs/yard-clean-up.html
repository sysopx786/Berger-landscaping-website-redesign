About yard clean-up

Take proactive steps to ensure your lawn's vitality and beauty for the upcoming year. By removing weeds, raking fall leaves, and maintaining garden beds, you can prevent debris buildup throughout the season and into winter.

All FAQs by service
What does a yard clean-up include?

Removing weeds, raking leaves, and tidying garden beds to prevent debris buildup and prepare your lawn for the year ahead.

When should I schedule a clean-up?

Spring and fall are the most popular times, but we can help whenever your yard needs a reset.

Why is a fall clean-up important?

Clearing leaves and debris before winter helps prevent mold, fungus, and pests, and protects your lawn through the cold months.

Why do a spring clean-up?

It clears winter debris and gets beds and lawn ready for healthy growth.

Do you remove the debris from my property?

Yes. We haul away what we clear.

Do you clean out garden beds?

Yes. Maintaining garden beds is part of a clean-up.

Can you handle an overgrown yard?

Yes. Tell us about the condition and we'll quote the work. Photos help.

Is a clean-up a one-time service or ongoing?

Either. You can book a single clean-up or fold it into a regular maintenance plan.

Can you also trim shrubs and bushes during the clean-up?

Yes, trimming can be added. See our Trimming service.

Do you remove fallen branches?

Yes. Branch and yard-debris removal is part of what we do. Very large limbs may need a separate quote.

Do you do clean-ups for rental or vacant properties?

Yes. Contact us with the address and what's needed.

Do I need to be home?

No, as long as we can access the yard.

How much does a clean-up cost?

It depends on yard size and condition. Call or text (610) 420-3338 for a quote.

Will a clean-up help my lawn?

Yes. Removing weeds and debris lets the lawn breathe and helps it stay healthy into the next season.

Can you combine a clean-up with leaf removal or mulching?

Yes. We can bundle services into one visit.
